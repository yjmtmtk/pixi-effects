Drag to turn · click a colour
Drag the sneaker to turn it (left / right to spin, up / down to tilt); let go and the turntable carries on. Double-click to put it back. Click a colour dot (or the colourway name) to switch between the two colourways. Scrubbing or exporting plays the plain 10-second turntable, in the colourway that is chosen.
