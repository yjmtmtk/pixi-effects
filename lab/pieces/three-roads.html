Click a signpost to choose your road

The film stops at the fork and waits for a click on one of the three signposts; what you click decides the last nine seconds. Untouched, it is an ordinary 15-second video (the middle road): the download button in the player bar, or pixi-effects render three-roads.html -o three-roads.mp4, exports it straight through. One ending on its own: --var path=0 (sea), 1 (stars), 2 (lights), or ?path=0 in the address.
