# pixi-effects pitfalls — what actually went wrong

Collected by having fresh AI sessions build pieces from the docs alone: first six (kinetic type, news lower-third, slideshow, 2.5D showcase, three.js title, bar chart), then a portfolio of 16 more (items 32 and up), made by three different models. Each entry is a real stumble. **Status**: `fixed` = the library now handles or warns; `docs` = nothing can catch it, so remember it; `hand-roll` = the DSL has no feature, see the workaround.

A good habit that catches most of these: after `init`, read `window.__logs` (see `ai/template.html`) — every pixi-effects warning says what to change.

## Time

1. **Keyframe `at` starts at 0 when THIS layer appears** *(fixed in 0.3.0; earlier versions measured from the composition and failed silently)*. A layer at `at: 2` with a keyframe `at: 0` animates at global 2 s. Negative `at` counts back from the layer's end. A keyframe that starts after its layer ends now warns ("never plays").
2. **A layer's own `at` and a transition's `at` are composition time** (seconds from the parent composition's start). Only keyframes are layer-local.
3. **Layers are hidden, not removed, after `[at, at+duration)`** and keep their last values. Z-order is array order (later = on top). To switch scenes, stack scenes with `at`/`duration`, each starting with an opaque full-screen rect. *(docs)*
4. **Transitions need overlapping lifespans.** Both layers must be alive for the whole window: scene `i` starts at `i·(L−T)`, the transition's `at` is the next scene's start, total = `n·(L−T)+T`. A scene that ends while the transition is still running leaves a gap. *(docs)*
5. **Sound effects need no file at all:** `{ type: 'audio', sfx: 'pop', at }` — the layer lasts exactly as long as the sound. **Audio length of a FILE.** Omit `duration` on a one-shot clip: the layer lasts exactly as long as the file (nothing to measure; the only sample file is `examples/_assets/bgm.mp3`, 6 s). `loop: true` with no `duration` lasts until the composition ends. An explicit `duration` longer than the clip goes silent after the clip unless `loop: true` (`bgm.mp3` is 6 s) and warns. Volume fades work like any keyframe: `volume: 0` + `{ at: 0, to: { volume: v }, duration: 2 }` + `{ at: -2, to: { volume: 0 }, duration: 2 }` (the last 2 s only; `set` is a jump). *(fixed: volume keyframes used to ramp from the previous keyframe's end, so the music faded across the whole piece)*
6. **Snap times to the frame grid (`Math.round(t*30)/30`) when two things must stay locked** (a bar and its label). Drive both from one JS easing function with per-frame `set` keyframes rather than mixing a tween with computed positions. *(docs)*
7. **Pick eases on purpose.** `expo.in` stays tiny until the very end (a "flood" circle never reaches the corners — use a bigger radius and `power2.in`). A zoom transition with `power2.out` looks like a cut because the incoming scene is nearly opaque after 40 %. Default is linear. *(docs)*
8. **`render()` runs in roughly real time** (6 s of 720p ≈ 6 s). Listen to `movie.on('progress', e => e.progress)` (0–100). *(docs)*

## Layout and anchors

9. **Defaults differ by type.** rect/circle/ellipse are centred on `x,y`; text and image are top-left; a composition has no anchor — use `pivotX/pivotY` and set `x,y` to where the pivot should land. For a bar growing from its base use `anchorY: 1` (or `anchorX: 0`). *(docs)*
10. **Shape geometry may be at the top level or in `initial`** (`width height radius anchorX …`). *(fixed: `initial` used to be silently ignored)*
11. **Angles are degrees** — `rotation`, `skew*`, `rotationX/Y` — and `rotation: -0.2` is almost no rotation. *(docs)*
12. **Text**: anchor 0.5 centres the line *box*, not the cap height (an underline under serif or italic text needs ~`0.9·size` below the centre: `0.46·size` touches the descenders of a g or y). Arial Black is ~0.8·size wide per glyph, monospace ~0.6·size; leave ~7 % for hold-scale. `style` accepts any PixiJS TextStyle field: `letterSpacing`, `wordWrap` + `wordWrapWidth`, `lineHeight`, `stroke`, `dropShadow`, `padding`. A glow (`dropShadow` with `blur`) is clipped unless `padding ≥ 2·blur`. *(docs)*
13. **`w` / `h` in expressions** are the layer's own size. For text they now describe the styled text (`x: '-w'` scrolls a marquee fully out). *(fixed)*
14. **Masks live in the parent's coordinate space and do not follow the masked layer.** Reveal by growing the mask's `width` from a fixed edge (`anchorX: 0`); exit by tweening `x` and `width` together; slide the content separately. *(docs)*
15. **`line` / `polygon` / `path` use plain canvas coordinates** when you give no `x,y` (they used to be drawn around (0,0)); with `x,y` those place the shape's centre. Stroke colour and width go in `initial`. A background pill must be sized from its text (~0.58 × fontSize per glyph + padding): `inspect` does not check text against shapes. *(fixed)*
15b. **`filterArea` is in the layer's own coordinates** (origin = its local origin, e.g. a circle's centre), not the parent's. A blurred shape is clipped to its bounding box without it: `filterArea: { x: -(r+260), y: -(r+260), width: 2*(r+260), height: 2*(r+260) }`. `threeD` layers pad automatically. *(docs)*
16. **The player bar overlays the bottom ~60 px of the canvas** in browser screenshots (not in the exported video). `movie.snapshot()` / `movie.contactSheet()` never include it; keep captions above it anyway. *(fixed: use the built-in snapshot tools)*

## Missing features (hand-roll)

17. **Gradients / vignettes**: use shape `fillGradient` (linear or radial, stops may have alpha). Earlier sessions hand-rolled canvas images or dozens of stacked bands. *(fixed)*
18. **Counters**: `text: '{value}'` + animate `value` (with `format`). Other text content cannot change over time. Earlier sessions generated one text layer per number (40+ per bar). *(fixed)*
19. **Loops**: `repeat` (finite) / `yoyo` / `repeatDelay` on any keyframe; infinite repeats are rejected with a warning. *(fixed)*
20. **No per-frame expression time.** Expressions are evaluated once at build. A camera orbit is `orbit()`; a shake or drift is `wiggle()`, travel along a path is `followPath()`, and any other curve (waves, spirals) must be sampled into short linear keyframes (~0.1 s) in a JS loop. **Randomness must be seeded** (`rand(seed)` / `noise(x, seed)` in expressions, `random(seed)` in JS): `Math.random()` makes playback and the exported file disagree. *(partly fixed)*
21. **Per-letter / per-word text is `animateText()` (one call: a layer per piece, a wave in and out) or `splitText()` by hand (one layer per piece, positions measured for you); a group / parent is a `{ type: 'null' }` layer + `parent: 'name'`, no caption-linked-to-slide.** One layer per letter; many layers; seeded random loops (`random(seed)`). *(hand-roll)*
22. **Colour tweens look muddy in RGB** (red→green passes through brown). Use `colorSpace: 'oklab'` or `'oklch'` on the layer. In `oklch`, a nearly grey end (chroma < ~0.08, e.g. `#9a9ab4`) borrows the other end's hue, so grey → yellow no longer flashes through pink. *(docs / fixed)*
23. **Generated images**: `canvas.toDataURL()` as an asset `src` works. Make them ≥ canvas size or `kenBurns` zooms look soft. *(docs)*

## 2.5D / three.js

24. **`threeD` is required** for `z`, `rotationX`, `rotationY` (they warn otherwise). Camera props go in `initial`/keyframes, never on the camera object. `+z` is toward the viewer. *(fixed: warns)*
25. **A layer at/behind the camera plane is hidden** — the default camera sits at `z = (H/2)/tan(fov/2)` ≈ 989 at 720p, fov 40 (≈ 808 at fov 48, lower at higher fov). Keep every layer's `z` below it. *(fixed: warns once)* **It is hidden whole when ANY corner of it passes the camera plane**: a big plane tilted with `rotationX` / `rotationY` (a ground plane, an orbit ring) vanishes as soon as one corner swings behind the camera (the warning then says a corner reaches the plane). Tilt it less, shrink it, or keep the camera clear of its depth range. *(docs / fixed: warns)*
26. **Under a dolly zoom only the `z = 0` plane stays put**; everything nearer balloons and clips. Headline at `z: 0`, near cards `z ≤ ~70`. Perspective also pulls far layers toward the centre: put far cards at the outer x, near cards inward, and keep ~10 % margin for an orbit sweep. `orbit({ dollyZoom: { from, to } })` does both together. *(docs)*
27. **Combining orbit and dolly zoom**: `orbit()` specifies `z`, so it stops following `fov` — write your own `fov` and `z = (H/2)/tan(fov/2)` keyframes for the zoom. *(docs)*
28. **Nearer `threeD` layers sort on top of text** — lay out around them; equal `z` keeps array order. A card (rect + icon + label that move together) is a `composition` with `threeD: true`. A final call-to-action or overlay should be a plain 2D layer (last in the array): it ignores the camera, and fade the 3D scene and headline out before it appears (a dim rect alone leaves them visible). *(docs)*
29. **three.js**: the layer clips at its own rectangle — size it to the area you want and pull the camera back (`z ≈ 5.4` for a radius-1 torus knot at fov 50). Metal needs an environment map (`PMREMGenerator(ctx.renderer)`); `three/addons` is not in the importmap. Route `three` through the importmap so there is one copy. Masks/transitions/`filterArea` are not supported on `threeD` layers. *(docs)*

## Tooling (agent harness)

30. **A scene change must keep the outgoing scene alive until the covering flood / wipe has finished**, and the next scene starts when it is complete; the flood circle must reach the corners (radius ≥ half the diagonal) and use `power2.in`, not `expo.in`. *(docs)*
30b. **Unknown option names now warn** (`contactSheet({ cols: 2 })` → did you mean `columns`; `init({ fps })` → `frameRate`). Layer names show up in `inspect` paths, so give layers a `name`. `inspect` ignores faint (alpha < 0.3) layers and text whose x / y is animated (tickers), so a scrolling marquee is not reported as cut off. Pixi may print `[BindGroup] … destroyed while still bound` warnings at `movie.destroy()`: harmless (playback and seeking never warn). *(docs)*
30c. **Look with the built-in tools, not OS screenshots**: `await movie.contactSheet({ count: 6, as: 'dataURL' })` returns one labelled image of the whole animation; `await movie.inspect(frame)` returns every layer's canvas bounds and `issues`. If you do use a browser tool, `screenshot` needs an **absolute path**, macOS `sed -i` needs `''`, and a static server may redirect `/x.html` → `/x` and drop `?query`. Keep the template's `try/catch` around `init` so failures land in `window.__logs`. Check ≥ 5 timestamps including mid-transition. *(fixed: tools built in)*
31. **Check audio with `movie.inspectAudio()`**: every sound measured on its own (`sound.length`, `peakDb`, `loudestAt`, `brightnessHz`), when it plays, loudness and brightness per 0.1 s window, and `issues` (the mix was limited, a layer is inaudible, an sfx is cut off by the end). Decoding a render by hand is no longer needed: `render()` encodes the same mix, on time in mp4 / mov / webm / mkv. *(fixed)*

31b. **A mistyped `ease` now warns** (`ease: 'power3.outt'` → did you mean `power3.out`). It used to run silently as GSAP's default (`power1.out`), so the motion changed and nothing said so.
31c. **`draft` and `scale` make the file smaller, not the drawing faster** (measured: a 48 s 1080p film exports in about the same time as a draft; the file is 7× smaller). To save time, export a part (`range` / `--range` / `--scene`), look at single moments (`check --no-export --at …`), and keep motion blur off while you iterate.

## Round 2 — found by building 16 portfolio pieces (examples/gallery)

32. **Seeking is safe now** *(fixed)*: a `set` keyframe (counter value, text fill, image tint, shape style) is undone when you seek back before it, and shapes are redrawn before they are culled, so a rect growing from `width: 0` at the left edge draws after any jump seek (contact sheet, snapshot, scrubbing, export). The old workarounds (`width: 0.01`, a one-frame `to` instead of `set`) are not needed.
33. **A mask starts and ends with the layer it masks** *(fixed)*, so its keyframes count from that layer's start. A mask with an explicit `at` keeps composition time. Before, a mask `at: 0` reveal had finished before a layer that appeared at 8 s, and the layer stayed invisible without any warning.
34. **`from` and `from+to` show their start value from the LAYER's start**, not from the keyframe's `at` (GSAP behaviour): a delayed fade-in `{ at: 2, from: { alpha: 0 }, to: { alpha: 1 } }` needs no `initial.alpha`. To keep the current value until `at` and then jump, use `set`. *(docs)*
35. **One `Movie` per page.** Creating a second Movie (for an isolated repro) and destroying it breaks the first (`null.clear`). Reload instead. *(docs)*
36. **Multi-line text is centre-aligned by default** (`style.align: 'left'` for left-aligned paragraphs). *(docs)*
37. **Composition size.** A 2D composition does not clip its children (a child outside `width × height` still draws), but its rectangle is the culling area: if the whole rectangle is off the canvas the group is skipped even when a child is on screen — keep it near what it draws, or canvas-sized. A `threeD` composition (a card) IS clipped at its `width × height` (it is rendered into a texture that size): size it for the soft shadow too. A growing `threeD` shape or text is fine: its texture is resized in place (it used to warn "destroyed while still bound"). *(measured; docs / fixed)*
38. **Under an orbit the side the camera swings toward becomes the NEAR side**: a "far" card at `z: −200` can be drawn bigger than 1× on that side and run off the frame (`inspect` only checks text). Pull those cards inward / lower `z`, and check a frame at the extreme of the sweep. After `orbit()` ends the default camera returns, so to hold the final pose add a camera layer that starts there. Near particles balloon under a dolly zoom: cap particle `z` at about +150, or one becomes a fake full stop. *(docs)*
39. **`fillGradient` stops span the whole unmasked shape**, not the visible part: under a mask, place the stops for the visible fraction (e.g. 0.64→1). A translucent shape over near-black reads as grey: put the alpha in the gradient stops. *(docs)*
40. **Rotate a polygon / path / line about a chosen point with `pivotX` / `pivotY`** (in the shape's own canvas coordinates; put the same point in `x, y`): `{ points, initial: { pivotX: 600, pivotY: 300, x: 600, y: 300, rotation: 180 } }` turns about the vertex (600, 300) — measured. Without a pivot the shape turns about the centre of its bounds. `anchorX` / `anchorY` do nothing on these shapes (they are for rect / circle / ellipse). *(docs; the gallery wrapped every polygon in a composition unnecessarily)*
41. **Glow a whole set of layers** by putting them in one `composition` with a blur filter and `filterArea` (only a composition carries a filter for its children): wide enough for the blur, and the set is built once, so the extra copy is just a second group. *(docs)*
42. **Transition looks:** the default `dissolve` reads as a hard black-and-white camouflage and `scale: 220` as static; `scale: 90, smoothing: 0.5` is a soft film dissolve. The default edge softness of `iris` (0.02) and `wipe` blurs flat graphics at 720p: `smoothing: 0.004`–`0.01` gives a clean edge. *(docs)*
43. **Layers built in a loop need their z-order planned**: anything pushed to the array after the loop is drawn over what the loop made (rings crossed over a header). Keyframes on different properties of one layer may overlap in time. Keep a subject in the upper ~60 % of a source image if a caption will sit under it (`inspect` does not look at picture content). *(docs)*
44. **Zero-pad a counter with `format: { pad: 2 }`** (`18:42:{value}` shows `18:42:05`). A `{value}` placeholder that nothing sets or animates prints 0 and now warns: `{value}` is reserved, there is no way to print it literally. *(fixed)*
45. **The same mistake in a loop warns once**: the warning "keyframe starts after the layer ends" is summarised after three layers. The usual cause is an off-by-one (`f <= frames` instead of `f < frames`) that puts the last sample exactly at the layer's end. *(fixed)*
46. **Still not in the DSL (hand-roll):** shared masks (one mask for several layers), `zIndex` (stack order is list order). *(Earlier round-two notes listed draw-on strokes, changing text, jitter and particle emitters here: they are built in now: `trimEnd`, `set: { text }` / `visibleChars`, `wiggle()`, `particles()`.)*
46b. **Blend modes exist now** (`blendMode: 'add' | 'screen' | 'multiply'` on any layer, threeD layers and compositions included; a composition's children inherit it). Additive white cores no longer read grey on a coloured background. It also works on a layer with `filters` (a blurred glow with `add` / `multiply`): the mode is applied by the last filter, because a container's blend inside a filter pass multiplied against nothing and rendered black. With `add`, use dim saturated colours: overlaps sum, and near-white sums to pure white. *(fixed)*
47. **Browser tooling: prefer `node ai/tools/check.mjs page.html`** — it runs its own private Chrome, so none of the following can bite. If you drive a browser by hand instead: the `agent-browser` daemon is shared, so a `contactSheet` can come back from another session: after saving an image, look at it and confirm it is YOUR piece. In zsh a variable holding `agent-browser --session x` is not split into words (use a small wrapper script). `inspect` now clips overlaps to the visible area, ignores the two scenes of a running transition, treats text inside a moving (panned) composition as moving, and does not report text scaled to 0 as "no size"; it still does not compare text with picture content or shapes.
48. **A composition's `alpha` is applied to each child separately, not to the flattened group** (measured: two overlapping opaque circles in a composition at `alpha: 0.5` show the lower one through the upper one in the overlap). Fading a group that has overlapping children therefore shows the overlap; fade the children themselves, or keep overlapping parts out of the group. Expressions have no way to read a sibling's size: measure text with `canvas.measureText` (the font string Pixi uses) and compute pill widths in JS. *(docs)*
49. **sfx timing and level.** An sfx layer's `duration` is the sound's length (it stretches, it does not trim). Align the loud point: a `riser` peaks at its end, a `swoosh` 0.16 s in. Many sounds at the same instant add up: above 0.9 the mix is limited and a warning names the layers to turn down. Preset names, knobs and voice fields are closed lists — a guess (`whoosh`, `ding`, `impact`, `sawtooth`, `lp`, `decay`) warns with the right word. *(fixed: warns)*
50. **An image (or video) works as a mask, `maskInverted` included** *(fixed)*: PIXI hides a sprite mask with `renderable = false` and the lifespan toggles used to switch it back on, so the mask picture was drawn over the layer (a white disc) and `maskInverted` looked ignored. Use the picture's alpha (or red channel) as the shape: an opaque word or logo on a transparent canvas image is a text-shaped mask with real glyph edges. A `text` layer also works as a mask (cut to its glyphs; measured with a heavy sans `I`). *(docs / fixed)*
51. **Arcs and rings are `shape: 'arc'`** (`radius`, `innerRadius`, `startAngle`, `endAngle`, degrees; 0° = 3 o'clock, clockwise). A progress ring is an arc with `startAngle: -90` and an animated `endAngle` (−90 → 270); `strokeCap: 'round'` rounds BOTH ends. Fill it for a pie slice, add `innerRadius` for a donut slice (a 120-polygon donut is no longer needed). Style (`strokeColor`, `strokeWidth`, `fillColor`) goes in `initial`; `strokeCap` / `strokeJoin` may be top level. *(fixed)*
52. **Measure and split text with `measureText` / `splitText`, not by hand.** `splitText(text, style, { by, x, y, align })` returns each piece's x / y / width in the renderer's own font metrics (kerning kept; verified: a whole line vs the same line in per-letter layers differs only by anti-aliasing). The pieces are for text layers with the default top-left anchor and the same `style`. Load a web font before measuring (`await document.fonts.load('24px "Name"')`) or the fallback font's widths are used. Size a pill from `measureText(...).width + padding` instead of `0.58 × fontSize × length`. *(fixed)*
53. **Draw-on and typewriter need no masks or per-letter layers.** `trimStart` / `trimEnd` (0–1 of the outline, animatable, every shape but `arc`) draw a stroke on: `trimEnd: 0` plus a keyframe `to: { trimEnd: 1 }`. The fill is not trimmed, so fade `fillAlpha` in afterwards; use `strokeCap: 'round'`. Text changes over time with `set: { text }` (swap at a time) and `visibleChars` (typewriter; left-align it so it grows rightward). A `to: { text }` cannot tween a string and warns. *(fixed)*
54. **A deck page lasts until its last stop plus the transition.** `deck()` lays pages out one after another in page-local time and a `transition` overlaps them: a page shorter than its last stop (or shorter than the transition) loses its final step or flashes. A step is a layer that starts at the previous stop (`at: <previous stop>`), so the thing that appears on a press is fully visible when the movie stops. The transition option is `kind`, not `type`. If the last stop is mid-exit on paper, flag the stop you want in the PDF: `{ at, pdf: true }` (in `deck()`: `stops: [1.2, { at: 3, pdf: true }]`), and `pdf: false` keeps one out.
55. **Presenter and Controller both listen to click and keys.** Use the `Presenter` for a talk (or let the Controller's Present button start one); do not run both on the same canvas yourself. `movie.play()` ignores stops, but the Controller's play button / Space / click-on-picture play to the next stop and pause (`pauseAtStops: false` plays through); `movie.next()` plays to the next stop (a second press skips to it), `prev()` jumps back (nothing plays in reverse).
56. **Analyse audio before `init`, and give it the movie's frame rate.** `const env = await audioEnvelope('music.mp3', { frameRate })` is async; `react()` is pure and bakes the keyframes, so there is no per-frame callback. A custom `bands` list replaces the defaults (include `bass` if you want it; beats are picked there). No file yet: `bpmEnvelope(120, { duration })` is a stand-in with a kick on every beat. Offset the music with `audioOffset` when the audio layer does not start at 0.
57. **Review a deck by its stops, not by its timeline.** `pixi-effects-check` writes `stops.png` (one picture per stop, in order) for a page with `stops`; the picture at a stop is what the audience reads while it waits. A stop that lands a few frames before an animation ends shows it half-finished (also in the PDF): `movie.inspectStops()` / the check's review list names such stops; give an animation a little more time than its step (e.g. the next stop 0.3 s after it ends).
58. **`music` counts BEATS, and every track has to add up.** A token's `:n` is beats (a quarter note), a 4/4 bar is 4: a bar of `c4:1 e4:1 g4:1` is a beat short and every later bar drifts. Keep all tracks the same length (the music lasts the longest; a track of rests `'_:16'` sets a length) and check the printed length with `movie.inspectAudio()`. 6/8 is a bar of 3 beats (dotted quarter `:1.5`, eighth `:0.5`) with `grid: 2`; `grid: 3` is triplet eighths in 4/4.
59. **A groove that must enter late needs `from`, not a long string.** `drums: [{ kick: 'x...' }, { from: 8, snare: '....x...' }]`. Patterns repeat; a drum object is a loop of its longest string. A `crash` on the last bar: its own section `{ from: 28, crash: 'x' }`.
60. **Build-ups are layers and `vol` points, not louder notes.** A track's `vol` can be `[[0, 0], [8, 0.7]]` (a fade over 8 beats); the pad's `attack` can be 2–3 s; enter instruments with rests. Per-note `!` and `,` only accent and soften.
61. **Music is a bed: `volume` 0.5–0.8 under speech or sfx.** At `volume: 1` it peaks at −6 dBFS (an sfx peaks at −12). A layer shorter than the music cuts it with a fade; longer is silent after it unless `loop: true`. Do not also add an mp3 bed.
62. **Chord names are voiced close around octave 3**: use `[c3 g3 e4]` for an exact voicing or `Am7/e` for a bass note; `swing` only moves notes that start on an off-beat eighth (`.5`) or sixteenth (`.75`); the bell and music box ring for `ring` seconds whatever the written length (shorten `ring` for fast bell lines); the synth is a handy bed, not an orchestra: keep tracks to 3–6, vary the bars, and end on the tonic.
63. **`animateText()` / `splitText()`: `x` and `y` are pixel numbers, and `y` is the TOP of the line.** An expression such as `x: 'GW/2'` throws (the letters are measured once, at build time): write `x: W / 2`. The pieces are centred half a line below `y`, so a line drawn under the text goes at `y + lineHeight` (about 1.2 × `fontSize`), not at `y`; look at the contact sheet, the layout check cannot see an underline through a word. (`check` writes into `check-out/` of the current folder unless you pass `--out`.)
