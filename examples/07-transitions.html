07 · transitions

Eight named scenes chained by all seven transition kinds. Each scene is a full-canvas color with its label centred. Transitions sit on the composition itself, not on individual sequences — they reference siblings by name.

  • crossfade — alpha cross-dissolve
  • wipe — soft directional reveal (shader mask)
  • iris — circular reveal from / to a point
  • slide — both sequences move together; new scene comes from the opposite side
  • dip — A fades out, then B fades in; whatever is behind shows through
  • zoom — punch-in / punch-out scale + fade
  • dissolve — Perlin-noise pixel reveal, deterministic by seed
