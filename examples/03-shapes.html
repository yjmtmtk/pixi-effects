03 · shapes

Every type: 'shape' primitive in one frame. Each tile shows one kind, animates a property that's particular to it, and labels itself.

  • Six shape kinds: rect, circle, ellipse, line, polygon, path (SVG)
  • Style props: fillColor, fillAlpha, strokeColor, strokeWidth
  • Scalar geometry is animatable: radius, width, cornerRadius tween per-frame
  • colorSpace: 'oklch' for vivid hue sweeps that don't pass through grey
  • anchorX: 0 trick: left-anchored progress bar grows rightward
