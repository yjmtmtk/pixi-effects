## Transparent cutouts (`cutouts/`)

`teapot-*.webp` are photographs of objects in the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection (CC0 1.0), uploaded to Wikimedia Commons and checked on the file pages on 2026-10-06. The subject was lifted from its grey studio background (macOS Vision foreground-instance mask), cropped to the object, scaled to about 900 px and saved as lossless-alpha WebP (64–94 KB each; the same cutouts as PNG are 650–700 KB). No credit is required; the credit lines are the Met's.

| File | Object | Source page |
|---|---|---|
