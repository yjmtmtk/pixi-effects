transitions — text-only sequences

Same chain as transitions.html, but each scene is just a Text sequence (no full-canvas background). This shows the limit of the wipe / iris masks: they apply per-sprite to the text glyphs, so during the smoothstep zone the outgoing label remains visible in the band where the new label hasn't fully revealed yet.

For clean scene-to-scene transitions, wrap each scene in a composition with a full-canvas background (see transitions.html). Text-only is best paired with crossfade.
