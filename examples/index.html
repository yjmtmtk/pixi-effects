Examples

Read them in order

Each example is one HTML file that focuses on one feature area. Open it, read the source, copy what you need. The first one is the smallest composition that plays.

  1. 01helloThe smallest useful composition: one text layer that fades in, sits and fades out. Read this first.
  2. 02keyframes & expressionsOne text layer moves through every transform property; each keyframe shows what one knob does.
  3. 03shapesEvery shape type in one frame, each animating a property particular to it.
  4. 04mediaImage, video and audio together; a chroma-keyed video drops its green over a gradient picture.
  5. 05composition & maskNested compositions with their own coordinates, and masks: an avatar crop, an iris reveal, a punched-out hole.
  6. 06filtersChroma key, blur, colour matrix and community pixi-filters, every parameter animated by keyframes.
  7. 07transitionsEight named scenes chained by all seven transition kinds.
  8. 08presets & exportFour Ken Burns motions with crossfades, and a button that exports from code with movie.render().
  9. 09audioA music track and four short effects synced to the picture; the music ducks while an effect plays.
  10. 10three.js layerA rotating three.js torus knot composited as an ordinary layer.
  11. 11depth & camera2D layers placed in depth and seen through a camera, without three.js.
  12. 12title motionA 2.5D title from text, shapes and a camera: letters fly in from depth while the camera orbits.
  13. 13sound effectsEvery sound is synthesised from data: no audio files.
  14. 14draw-on strokes, changing textStrokes that draw themselves on, and text that types or changes over time.
  15. 15your own playerThe Controller is optional: build a player from the movie's public methods and events.
