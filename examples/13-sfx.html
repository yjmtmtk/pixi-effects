13 · sound effects

Every sound in this movie is synthesised from data — no audio files. An audio layer with sfx: 'pop' (or sfx: { preset: 'pop', pitch: 5, seed: 2 }) plays a preset at its at.

  • 0.5–10.5 s: the eleven presets, one per second; each tile lights up when its sound starts.
  • 11.5 s: a riser leads into a hit (at = hit − duration: a riser is loudest at its end), then three pops climb in pitch.
  • The layer's duration is the sound's length: omit it for the preset's own length.
  • 14 s: a custom sound from sfx: { voices: [...] } — one falling square wave.
